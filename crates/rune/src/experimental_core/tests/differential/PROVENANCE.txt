Provenance of this directory's case data

Files: differential_cases.tsv and programs/{operators,addendum,rebind,supplement,objfmt}.rn
(paths relative to this directory), derived from rnx replay receipts. The program
files remain unchanged. V2a removed the historical outcome columns from the case
table; its current SHA-256 is 62e274d441c6df52766ea558ef49a71291606c7dd9355f981d640e2e03252e66.
The original table's hash and grant are preserved below as historical provenance.

Origin:      the rnx repository (github.com/nilket-com/rnx) at revision
             bc286c817cae84e06a3254246bc4dc212b85fc26, paths
             probes/0184/reference/receipts/d29sf-replay1/attempt/{*.stdout[.gz],
             *.result.json[.gz], stage/*.tsv[.gz], stage/*.rn}, retained by commit
             72a677062f4983012e99dbfe9ee30b501b34923f.
Generator:   a deterministic script that reads the receipts only (kept with the M1 review
             records, not in this repository).
Fresh side:  outcomes of the candidate replay engine pinned at 515d4a779595d0d607ffec32048b8d2e718111b0,
             whose partitions are identical to D21's (3ffdb3da4d22fb7820f3a4103d8c179814acc7c6).
Stock side:  reference outcomes observed on the stock VM of fork bb8e69372353c50e271c9f115bc771c77aa6b83e
             by the rnx reference runners; error kinds are read as classes by the site tables of
             probes/0184/reference/OPERATOR_AMENDMENT.md and CANDIDATE_REPLAY.md (R1).
Re-expression: host::mark(x) -> mark(w, x) and host::t(n, x) -> t(w, n, x), with a
             caller-owned witness vector `w`.
