P11 plan: remove the formatter call during trusted standard-module insertion

Base: Rune c7aee7e072dd84604fe41c6d94270942e49da4c8.
Authority: Claude rune-v1 seq23206, following the read-only P11 Step0.
This is the third product record after M6 and carries M7.

The sole production caller, Context::insert_native_fn, currently calls
formatting::State::begin for every native function. During the private
StdFormatEpoch, begin returns immediately because trusted_install is true.
Move that check to the caller: call begin only when trusted_install is false.
Remove the trusted_install arm from begin. Its marked/certificate behavior,
copy-on-write order, allocation errors and modified-key decisions stay the
same. The guard still clears on normal return, error and unwind.

Keep numeric::State::invalidate_hash, pipeline, sort and insert-once conflict
transitions in their current order and byte-for-byte. The numeric state has
live certifications during standard-module construction, so skipping or
batching invalidation is not approved. No annotations, recipe, profile,
measurement protocol, layout or foreign product source changes belong here.

Test paired insertion sequences under a trusted epoch and an untrusted marked
reference. Observe numeric method, direct and CMP-alias decisions after each
protocol, item and associated insertion, including conflicts and a later
public install. Exercise both with_config stdio settings. Retain formatter
effective-key, certificate, copy-on-write, conflict, provenance, error and
unwind controls. Compare decisions, not private numeric bit layouts.

After implementation, run focused tests and then the complete Rune and Rnx
correctness gates under the reviewed host policy. Use V1c1 source, build,
seal, replay and record evidence for P11. Measure CGU1 standing rules and
the CGU16 M7 cells and K1 against M6, including the three carried cycle flags.
Report the actual per-call instruction change; 4,104 calls times 19 old
formatter instructions is 77,976 old instructions, not a net prediction.
If M7 fails, stop with attribution. Do not add numeric batching or a cost
allowance without another reviewed plan.
