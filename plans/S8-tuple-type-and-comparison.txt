S8: tuple type tests and comparison safety

Authority: S8 Step 0 live audit and rulings. Base: the V2a WIP branch. S8 is
the first product record after M7 and lands with V2a only after the joint
fresh-clone verification passes. Measure the standing CGU1 cost rules.

The legacy engine reports unit and tuple values as Tuple. The fresh engine
represents unit and nonempty tuples separately. Their public type hashes are
shared, but lowering currently checks the unit hash first, so source-level
`is Tuple` emits the exact-unit test: unit passes and nonempty tuples fail.
Internal patterns also use an exact-unit test; widening that operation globally
would change pattern semantics.

Keep both internal representations and leave the generic Op::Is behavior
unchanged. For source-level `is Tuple`, evaluate the operand once, emit the
existing exact-unit and exact-tuple tests, and combine them with boolean OR;
for `is not Tuple`, combine their negations with boolean AND. Preserve slot
lifetime, moved-value error order and all non-tuple and nominal type tests.
Verify a host-created empty heap tuple, unit and nonempty tuple, including
closed and open tuple patterns. Report the cost at tuple sites and the standing
CGU1 measurement rather than asserting a hot-path result in advance.

The fresh engine also admits tuple and unit cmp calls that fail at runtime with
Unsupported. Implement the stock lexicographic comparison for unit and tuple
receivers, including unit/tuple and tuple/tuple, with correct ownership, moved,
depth and error semantics. Unit compared with a non-tuple must produce the
stock Type error. If any dynamic path cannot be implemented safely, refuse it
at compile time with a sound proof; never let an admitted call fail Unsupported.

Each Step 0 audit case becomes a committed live dual-engine test or a small
literal design assertion. Narrow the existing cast-failure equivalence to stock
UnsupportedAs versus fresh InvalidCast for the same operand and target; do not
expand other error classes. Cover safe refusal by its kind and preserve exact
unit and tuple pattern behavior. Add a receiver by method sweep for cmp, eq,
partial_cmp, hash, clone and len over unit, empty and nonempty tuple, scalars,
Vec, Object, Option, Result and nominal values: admitted runtime Unsupported
is a failure. Include the five L1 regressions and remove their known-failing
markers only once they pass. Do not commit generated output as an oracle.

Run focused tests, the full L1 live corpus, Rune and rnx correctness, Miri for
changed helpers, the product CGU1 rules, and the joint isolated fresh-clone
one-command checks. Keep all test output in scratch.

Additional Step 0 ruling: the live sweep also found admitted cmp defects for
Vec, Option, Result and inclusive ranges. S8 covers every admitted cmp receiver,
not only tuples. The bounded structural walk may serve Vec, Option and Result,
with live tests for moved values, depth, self-referential vectors, allocation
failure and mixed elements. Implement stock-exact range comparison where proved,
or soundly refuse the unproved shape before execution. Sweep full, from, to,
exclusive and inclusive range forms. Nested floating-point NaN comparison must
preserve IllegalFloatComparison and operand bits; it is not an allowed mapping.
